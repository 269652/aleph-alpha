## PvP: zone-based risk escalation

Given how central [permadeath](death.md) already is, PvP stakes scale with
zone danger rather than being uniform everywhere — low-level/starting
areas stay safe, dangerous/high-level areas carry real open-world stakes.
Same shape as EVE's security-status zones or ARK's official-PvP-vs-safe
split, mapped onto this world's own biome/danger gradient
([world.md](world.md)).

- **Low-level areas: consensual/flagged PvP only.** Players are safe by
  default here — no ambient permadeath-relevant PvP without opting in
  (dueling, flagged contested zones, guild-war declarations from
  [factions.md](factions.md)). Once flagged, a fight carries the same
  life/soul-stone stakes as PvE death, so it still means something when it
  happens — it just can't be forced on someone going about their business
  in a starter zone.
- **Higher-level areas: full-loot open-world PvP.** In the more dangerous
  regions the world-sim and progression already gate behind higher
  character power (deep wilderness, endgame content, wherever
  [world bosses](worldbosses.md) and top-tier [resources.md](resources.md)
  live), PvP is unrestricted and losses are real — no flagging required,
  matching the raised stakes of everything else out there.
- This mirrors the ecosystem's own risk gradient (a lush starting biome
  vs. a harsh, resource-rich frontier) instead of being an arbitrary rule
  layered on top of it.
