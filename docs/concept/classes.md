## Classes

No hard class lock. "Class" is a lens over your build, not a cage — closer to
how Path of Exile's starting class barely constrains your final skill web, or
Guild Wars 2's attribute system, than to WoW's talent-tree-per-class model.

- **DNA resonance is soft affinity, efficiency-only** (see
  [dna.md](dna.md)): your rolled DNA gives a resonance score per archetype
  (e.g. 72% Mage, 45% Warrior, 20% Artisan). Higher resonance means faster
  leveling / bigger stat gains when investing in that archetype's skills —
  it never gates content. Any DNA can eventually learn any spell, craft any
  recipe, or master any archetype; a bad roll just makes the road longer.
  This keeps the DNA-reroll-then-pay-premium mechanic in dna.md as the
  "want it faster/better" lever, without also using DNA to lock players out
  of a playstyle they want to play.
- **Free respec.** Skill/spell investment (see [skills.md](skills.md),
  [magic.md](magic.md)) can be reallocated — archetype is a snapshot of your
  current build, not a permanent choice made at character creation.
- **Archetype picked at creation is a starting lens/tutorial path**, not a
  restriction — it determines your starting skills, gear, and which NPCs/
  quests initially treat you as "one of theirs," per [npc.md](npc.md)'s
  need-driven quest generation.

### Proposed archetype spread (wider than the classic trio, so every major
system has a class that's "about" it)

| Archetype | Core system it leans on | Reference points |
|---|---|---|
| **Warrior** | Melee/tank combat | Hammerwatch, Diablo |
| **Mage** | Spellcrafting DSL | Morrowind |
| **Ranger** | Ranged combat, wilderness tracking, vegetation concealment | ATLAS, classic MMO hunter |
| **Beastmaster** | Deep taming/breeding, pet combat | ARK, Pokémon trainer |
| **Artisan** | Crafting, building, resource specialization | Terraria, Stardew Valley |
| **Herbalist** | Medicine, sickness-curing, buff/support | Rune Factory, WoW druid/priest |
| **Overseer** | Hiring/instructing NPCs via the NPC DSL, logistics/economy | RimWorld colony leader, EVE industrialist |

Open for discussion — does this spread feel right, or should some of these
merge/split? (E.g. Ranger and Beastmaster overlap somewhat; Herbalist could
fold into Artisan instead of standing alone.)
