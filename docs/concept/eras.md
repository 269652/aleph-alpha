## Progression through eras (long-term vision)

Distinct technological eras exist on a planet: medieval → industrial
revolution → AI boom → space exploration boom, etc. A player can choose, at
some point, to end their current life and be reincarnated into a more
advanced era. This is a core long-term pillar of the vision but is **out of
scope for the MVP**, which takes place in a single era on a single world.

Open design questions (see [overview.md](overview.md#open-questions-to-resolve-during-mvp-work-not-blocking-day-1)):
permadeath semantics and how this interacts with [death.md](death.md), what
if anything carries over between eras, per-player vs per-server era state.

### Era transitions can be boss-gated

Per [worldbosses.md](worldbosses.md#era-gated-bosses) (2026-07-16 brainstorm):
advancing to the next era isn't purely a timer or milestone checklist — a
signature emergent world boss, the defining threat of the current age, can be
one of the required triggers a reincarnating character (or the world, per the
per-player/per-server question above) must clear first.
