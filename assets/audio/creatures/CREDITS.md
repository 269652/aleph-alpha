## Real-world substitutions (named, not silent)

- **`boar.ogg` is a domestic pig recording**, not a wild boar specifically
  -- both are *Sus scrofa* (wild boar is the ancestral/undomesticated
  form of the same species), and Wikimedia had no isolated wild-boar
  vocalization but did have a clean, short domestic pig grunt. A
  genuinely wild-boar-specific recording would be a real, if minor,
  upgrade if sourced later.
- **`bee.ogg` is a bumblebee recording** (German "Hummel"), shared by both
  `honeybee` and `wild_bee` -- a live worker bee's buzz doesn't read as a
  distinct pitch/texture between a managed honeybee and a solitary wild
  bee at this game's scale, so one real recording standing in for both is
  a reasonable simplification, not a wrong-species swap the way a
  different insect's buzz would be.

## Species deliberately left out

Real-world grounding decided which species get a call at all, the same
discipline `docs/concept/soundscape.md`'s pillar 3 already applies to
ambient layers: ants, decomposer bugs, millipedes, caterpillars,
earthworms, fish (goldfish/bluegill/trout/koi), and butterflies
(monarch/swallowtail/blue morpho) are all inaudible to a nearby human in
reality, so none of them get a fabricated sound here just for uniform
coverage.
